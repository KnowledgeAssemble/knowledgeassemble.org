About KnowledgeAssemble

We are exploring what it could look like if knowledge were easier to create, connect, explore, and share.

01 Foundational Insight

Much of the world's knowledge exists inside books, applications, websites, databases, institutions, and proprietary platforms.

These systems are useful, but the knowledge within them is often difficult to move, remix, or experience in new ways. Silos turn intellectual breakthroughs into isolated gardens.

KnowledgeAssemble explores open systems that separate knowledge from the software used to present it.
