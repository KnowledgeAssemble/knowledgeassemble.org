## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an optimistic, intellectually vibrant, and approachable ethos tailored for learners, educators, and researchers. It bridges institutional academic credibility with the energetic, encouraging atmosphere of modern digital learning environments. The visual language rejects traditional bureaucratic education tropes—drab slates, sterile administrative tables, and unyielding square grids—in favor of warm humanism, buoyant structural clarity, and tactile warmth.

The brand persona is an inspiring mentor: patient, deeply informed, encouraging, and clear. Visual weight leans toward open breathing room, organic modular cards, soft ambient paper textures, and deliberate high-contrast typographic hierarchy to sustain focus over extended study sessions.

## Colors
The color palette anchors on an organic, non-fatiguing warm cream foundation (`#fbfbfa` canvas with `#f4f5f0` surface tiers), eliminating harsh glare while maintaining high contrast against ink-toned text (`#1e293b`). 

- **Primary (`#1d4ed8`)**: Royal Indigo serves as the key interactive beacon for decisive user actions, primary links, and high-order focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#0e7490`)**: Warm Deep Teal provides intellectual balance, applied to structural indicators, active navigation, and secondary interaction surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: Supportive Forest Emerald conveys progress, mastery, validation, and completed academic milestones.
- **Accent Warmth (`#f59e0b` / `#ea580c`)**: Honey Amber and Coral provide dynamic highlights for streaks, badges, attention prompts, and playful celebratory states.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: A stepped sequence of warm off-white canvases (`#fbfbfa`), elevated card surfaces (`#ffffff`), and subdued divider fills (`#e7e8e1` to `#d9dad2`) ensure soft division without high-contrast friction. All foreground text meets strict WCAG AA contrast criteria on all assigned surface tiers.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the universal typeface across all tiers, providing humanist warmth, generous x-height, and open apertures that prevent reader fatigue during intensive coursework. 

Headings employ tight tracking and semi-bold to extra-bold weights to anchor page hierarchy clearly. Body text utilizes normal weight with open line heights (`1.6` to `1.65`) for effortless reading comprehension. Small labels and pill badges employ bold, tracked uppercase or title-cased treatments to retain structural presence without resorting to intrusive visual weight.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an 8px base spatial grid with an adaptive column framework:
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 40px outer margins. Content reaches a max reading width of 1280px to preserve comfortable scanlines.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px outer margins. Multi-column cards compress into dual-column grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer margins. Educational cards, metrics, and navigation rails reflow into unified vertical streams with sticky action points.

Component padding leverages the `space-*` scale: inputs and interactive pills default to `space-sm` vertical and `space-md` horizontal; modular card bodies scale dynamically from `space-md` to `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is built through soft ambient light and tinted surface transitions rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Base Layer**: The page canvas is rendered in warm parchment (`#fbfbfa`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Resting Cards & Modules)**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) background bounded by an ultra-gentle border (`1px solid #e7e8e1`) and an ambient, warm diffusion: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Hovered Cards & Flyouts)**: Elevated white cards respond on hover by scaling shadow reach: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.07)`, pairing a whisper of primary indigo tint with physical lift.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Overlays & Dialogs)**: Modal dialogs and major drawers use a dual shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(231, 232, 225, 0.8)` layered over a softened backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(251, 251, 250, 0.85)`).

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` establishes a welcoming, human-first feel across the interface. Standard buttons, text inputs, and micro-containers feature `0.5rem` (`8px`) corners. 

Content modules, assignment blocks, and interactive resource panels step up to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`). Tags, status chips, subject filters, and avatar frames adopt full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create playful, touch-friendly touchpoints that visually offset long-form text blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Royal Indigo (`#1d4ed8`) background, white text, `rounded-xl` geometry, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Includes a subtle bottom offset border (`1.5px solid #1e40af`) giving tactile reassurance. On hover: `#2563eb`.
- **Secondary**: Tinted soft teal (`#0e7490` with 8% opacity background), deep teal text (`#0e7490`), `rounded-xl`. On hover: 15% opacity background.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, ink text (`#334155`), `rounded-xl`. On hover: warm cream hover state (`#f4f5f0`).

### Chips & Badges
- Constructed with full pill borders (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding, paired with `label-sm` typography.
- **Subject Pills**: Warm cream ground with subtle color-coded dot indicators (e.g., Amber for Humanities, Teal for STEM, Indigo for Core Curriculum).
- **Status Pills**: Pastel-tinted backgrounds with rich foregrounds (e.g., Forest Green `#ecfdf5` background with `#059669` text for "Completed").

### Cards & Modules
- Clean pure white backgrounds framed with `1px solid #e7e8e1`, standard corner curvature of `rounded-xl` (`24px`).
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`). Feature header row with contextual student/educator pill tag and quick-action menu, followed by clear headline typography and progressive status meters.

### Form Inputs
- Background in pure white or elevated cream (`#fdfdfc`), encased in a crisp `1.5px solid #d9dad2` border with `rounded-lg` (`8px`) curvature.
- Active focus invokes a dual-ring glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)` with a solid `#1d4ed8` boundary. Placeholders use soft slate (`#94a3b8`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- `rounded-md` (checkbox) and `rounded-full` (radio) shapes sized at `20px × 20px`. Unchecked state features `#d9dad2` stroke; checked state smoothly transitions to `#1d4ed8` with crisp white SVG checkmarks and dot fills.

### Educational Specialty Components
- **Progress Trackers**: Pill-shaped horizontal track (`#e7e8e1`) filled with a soft gradient (`#10b981` to `#059669`), paired with milestone pins.
- **Callout Call-to-Thought**: Light cream note containers with a soft vertical bar in warm amber (`#f59e0b`), hosting reflection questions, quick formulas, or tutor tips.
